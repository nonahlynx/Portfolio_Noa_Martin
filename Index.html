About me

Hi! My name is Noa Martín and I'm a 2D/3D digital artist. I'm currently studying to become a higher technician in 2D/3D animation, videogames and interactive environments.

I'm from Seville, Spain and I love meeting new people, watching movies and series, playing video games, travelling and of course, drawing.

I'm passionate about visual storytelling, especially character design, concept art and 3D modelling.

I'm into deep, meaningful stories and I really enjoy exploring different artistic styles!

I'm always eager to learn new things and improve my skills :)
